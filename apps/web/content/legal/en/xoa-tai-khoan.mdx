## How to delete your account

You can do this yourself. You do not need anyone's permission.

1. Sign in on the app or the web.
2. Open your account settings.
3. Choose Delete account.
4. Confirm by following the steps on screen.

<DeleteAccountLink />

After deleting, you will be signed out. There is currently no way to restore the account yourself, so think carefully before you confirm.

## What happens right away

- Your profile, listings and groups are hidden from all public pages.
- Ride and game groups you created that are still open are cancelled. Members are notified.
- You leave the groups you are in. Pending join requests are also dropped.
- Your push notification tokens are removed, so you stop receiving notifications.
- Nobody can view your phone number through a listing any more.
- The account can no longer be used.

## What happens after 30 days

After 30 days, the system permanently deletes your data automatically. This runs daily in batches, so it may take slightly longer.

Data that is permanently deleted:

- Your profile and sign-in details, including your phone number or Google link.
- Your listings and groups.
- Profile pictures, listing photos and verification documents.
- Ratings by you and about you, reports you sent, saved listings, notifications and listing view history.

## What remains

Some data is not completely removed:

- **Messages:** messages and images you sent in chat stay in the other person's conversation. The sender name is removed, which means the messages are anonymised. If you wrote personal information in a message yourself, that text remains.
- **Search queries:** kept, but no longer linked to you.
- **Admin logs:** kept, with the person who took the action left blank.
- **Backups:** deleted data may remain in backups for about 7 days, then it is gone.
- **Contact form on the web:** not linked to an account, so it is not deleted with your account. If you want it handled, write to us.

## Before you delete

- Save any information you need, because you cannot get back in after deleting.
- Tell the people you are trading or travelling with.
- If you only want a break, you can turn off notifications or close your listings instead of deleting your account.

## If you cannot sign in

If you lost your phone number or cannot get into your account, write to us at <ContactEmail />. Give the phone number or email of the account. We may need to confirm that you own the account before we act. Do not send us an OTP code.

## If you only want to delete some data

You can close listings or edit your profile yourself. If you want other data deleted but want to keep your account, write to <ContactEmail />.
