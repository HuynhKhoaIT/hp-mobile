## Who we are

<AppName /> is operated by <Operator />. This policy covers the mobile app and the website, under Vietnamese law on personal data protection.

<AppName /> is an independent app. It does not belong to and does not represent Hoa Phat Group. The app does not connect to any company's HR data.

When you sign up, you agree to this policy. We store the time you agreed and the version you agreed to.

## Data we collect

- **Sign-in:** phone number (OTP), or Google account (name, email, profile picture).
- **Profile:** name, profile picture, the work site you choose, interests, department (optional), language.
- **Content:** listings, photos, listing location, ride and game groups, messages, chat images, ratings, reports.
- **Verification:** documents you send to apply for a badge.
- **Device and preferences:** push notification token, notification preferences.
- **Usage:** search queries, listing views.
- **Contact form on the web:** phone number or email, name, message and traffic source, if you choose to send them. To fight spam, we only store a one-way hash of your IP address.

## Why we use data

- To create accounts, sign you in, and show listings and groups.
- To suggest listings near your work site and based on your interests.
- To send notifications and let users message each other.
- To moderate content, fight scams and spam, and handle reports.
- To fix errors and improve the app.

## What other people can see

Public pages, including for people who are not signed in:

- Do **not** show your phone number.
- Do **not** show your full name. They show only your first name and the initial of your family name, for example "An N.".
- Show only a location rounded to about 300 m. Only you and moderation staff can see the exact location.
- Do show your profile picture, work site, badges, rating and joining date. Profile pictures and listing photos are public images.
- Group pages show the short names and profile pictures of members who have joined.

**Text you type yourself appears exactly as written.** Listing titles, descriptions and addresses, and group start and end points, are all public. Do not write a house number or phone number there if you do not want others to see it.

**Phone numbers on listings:** signed-in users can tap to see the account phone number of the person who posted a listing. Each person can do this at most 20 times per hour. Each view is logged. Blocked people cannot view it.

The phone number of a sports venue is a business number and may be shown publicly after approval.

## Messages

Only people in a conversation can read its messages in the app. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. The system checks messages automatically to block phone numbers and banned words. When there is a report, we may view the reported message in order to handle it.

## Verification documents

Documents are kept in private storage. Only you and moderation staff can view them. Documents are deleted automatically 30 days after they are approved or rejected.

## Service providers

We share only the data needed with the parties below. Some services only run when the related feature is turned on.

- **Supabase:** database, sign-in, file storage. Data is located in Singapore, which means outside Vietnam.
- **Vercel:** website hosting.
- **Expo:** sending push notifications.
- **Google:** Google sign-in; Cloud Translation when you tap the Translate button, sending only the listing title and description.
- **Goong:** maps and addresses.
- **SMS or Zalo ZNS provider:** sending OTP codes.
- **PostHog:** usage analytics.
- **Sentry:** error reporting, with personal data removed before sending.

## Cookies

The website always uses essential cookies to keep you signed in. Your browser also stores some preferences such as language, theme and listing drafts. On the website, PostHog analytics cookies run only after you agree. You can refuse and still use the website normally.

## Storage and deletion

- Data is kept while your account is active.
- When you delete your account, your profile, listings and groups are hidden right away and permanently deleted after 30 days. See the Delete account page for details.
- Messages you sent are anonymised, not removed from the other person's conversation.
- Violating content is rejected and kept, not deleted right away. Logs of staff actions are kept.
- Data sent through the contact form is not linked to an account. Write to us if you want this data handled.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data.
- **Correct** wrong data.
- **Delete** your data and account.
- **Withdraw your consent.**
- **Object** to the processing of your data.

You can do some of this yourself in settings: edit your profile, change notification preferences, delete your account. For other requests, write to <ContactEmail />. We may need to confirm that you own the account. Withdrawing consent may mean you can no longer use some features.

## Security

Access is controlled at the data layer. All staff actions are logged. Even so, no system is completely safe.
