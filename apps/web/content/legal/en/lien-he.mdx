## Report inside the app

If you see a fake listing, a scam or someone behaving badly, the fastest way is to tap Report right there. You do not need to write an email.

You can report:

- Listings.
- Ride and game groups.
- User profiles.
- Messages.

When you report, choose a reason: scam or deposit request, spam, inappropriate content, or another reason. You can add more detail.

Reports go to moderators. A listing reported by several people may be temporarily hidden while it is reviewed.

If you do not want someone to contact you any more, use the Block function.

## What to include in an email

To help us act quickly, please state clearly:

- What you need: reporting a bug, making a complaint, asking about data, or something else.
- The phone number or email used for your account.
- The link to the listing, group or profile involved.
- When it happened and what happened.
- Screenshots, if you have them.
- For app errors: which phone you use, and whether you use the app or the web.

Do **not** give an OTP code to anyone, even someone who claims to be support staff. Do not send identity documents by email. Verification documents should only be sent through the badge application in the app.

## Personal data requests

You can edit your profile and delete your account yourself in settings. For other requests, such as viewing, correcting or deleting data, withdrawing consent or objecting to data processing, write to the email above. We may need to confirm that you own the account.

## In an emergency

We are not an authority and we do not settle disputes for the parties. If you have lost money to a scam, are being threatened or are in danger, report it to the nearest police right away. Then report the listing or person in the app so that others are not harmed.
